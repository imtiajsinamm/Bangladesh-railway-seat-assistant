Seat Selection

Enter exactly as many ids as Number of Seats, in order (e.g. 4 seats → KHA-32, SCHA-21, UMA-3, DA-17). If one is taken, the nearest available seat next to it is used instead.

Ready to start

Selects seats only, then stops. You press CONTINUE PURCHASE and do passenger info, OTP and payment yourself.
